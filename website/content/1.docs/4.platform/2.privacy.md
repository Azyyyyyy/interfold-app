# Privacy policy

*Last updated: September 13, 2026*

This Privacy Policy applies to the Interfold app (the "app") for mobile devices, which is currently maintained
by Azyyyyyy (the "service provider" or "we/us") as a free service.

Interfold does **not** operate a hosted API. When you use the app, you connect it to an Interfold
server instance that you or someone else runs. Account data, system data, and other content you
enter in the app are stored on that instance under the control of whoever operates it, not by us.

## Information Collection and Use

The app collects some information on your device when you download and use it to properly provide its functionality.
This includes information such as:
- Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, sent to the Interfold server instance you configure
- Your app-specific authentication credentials for that instance

The app does **not** gather any information about:
- The precise location of your mobile device
- Most personally-identifiable information (your name, address, etc.)
- Anything not described in this document

Additionally, when you log in with an external service provider, that login is handled by the
Interfold server instance you connect to:
- Google: the instance may store your email address and associate it with your account.
- Apple: the instance may store your Apple user ID and, if you share it, your email address, and associate them with your account.
- Discord: the instance may store your Discord user ID and associate it with your account.
This information can be seen by other users of that instance.

## Third Party Access

The app records recent activity (such as API and connection events) **on your device** so you can review
it in Settings → Activity. That record stays on the device by default. The app does **not** send crash
reports or activity to a vendor crash service, and we do not receive that data.

If you turn on **Share activity with my Interfold server**, the app asks the Interfold instance you
configured for an OpenTelemetry (OTLP) address. If that instance advertises one, traces and logs may
be sent to **that collector** — the operator's configuration, not a vendor DSN we control. If the
instance does not advertise an address, or you leave the toggle off, nothing is exported.

You may also enter a custom OTLP endpoint to send the same on-device activity to a collector you
choose. Native process crashes (for example an iOS or Android app death) are not captured in this
activity log.

## Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the app by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstallation processes
available as part of your mobile device. To delete account data stored on an Interfold server instance, contact the
operator of that instance.

## Children

We do not use the app to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. We encourage all children to never
submit any personally identifiable information through the app and its affiliate services.

## Security

We have the utmost concern about safeguarding the confidentiality of information the app handles on your device.

We do not operate Interfold server databases. Data you store through the app lives on the Interfold instance
you connect to, and is the responsibility of that instance's operator.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time-to-time, for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by
updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are
advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes,
as continued usage is deemed approval of all changes.

This version of the Privacy Policy is effective as of September 13, 2026.

## Your Consent

By using the app, you are consenting to the processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and
as amended by us.
